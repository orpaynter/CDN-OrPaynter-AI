## 1) Full Document Library (Categorized)

### A. Core Commercial Contracts
1. **Master Services Agreement (MSA)**  
   Purpose: Governs platform licensing, services, allocation of risk, payment, IP, confidentiality, and core legal terms.  
   When used: Every enterprise/customer subscription or services engagement.
2. **Order Form / Statement of Work (SOW)**  
   Purpose: Defines commercial package, pricing, implementation scope, and service-specific deliverables.  
   When used: Per customer purchase or project phase.
3. **Professional Services Agreement / Implementation Addendum**  
   Purpose: Captures onboarding, integrations, migration, and advisory services with acceptance criteria and change-order controls.  
   When used: Any paid setup or custom workflow delivery.
4. **Mutual NDA**  
   Purpose: Protects pre-contract and partnership disclosures, demos, and technical exchanges.  
   When used: Before diligence, pilots, and strategic discussions.

### B. Product + Platform Terms
5. **Terms of Use (Website/Platform)**  
   Purpose: Public legal terms for users accessing websites/apps; includes acceptable behavior, disclaimers, and liability limitations.  
   When used: Public web/app access.
6. **Privacy Policy**  
   Purpose: Discloses how OrPaynter collects, uses, shares, and protects personal information across its websites, platform, and services.  
   When used: Public-facing notice required for all web/app/platform users; referenced by DPA, Terms of Use, and Minimum Viable Legal Stack.
7. **Acceptable Use Policy (AUP)**  
   Purpose: Prohibits abuse (fraud, scraping, credential sharing, malware, unauthorized claims manipulation).  
   When used: Incorporated into MSA, API terms, and public terms.
8. **API License + Developer Terms**  
   Purpose: Sets key management, rate limits, telemetry, deprecation, and security controls.  
   When used: Any API customer, partner, or integrator.
9. **SLA / Support Policy**  
   Purpose: Uptime targets, support windows, severity definitions, service credits, and exclusions.  
   When used: Paid support tiers and enterprise commitments.

### C. Data Protection + Security
10. **Data Processing Addendum (DPA)**  
    Purpose: Controller/processor terms, instructions, subprocessors, transfer controls, assistance obligations.  
    When used: Whenever personal data is processed for a customer.
11. **Information Security Exhibit**  
    Purpose: Baseline technical and organizational controls, encryption, access, vulnerability handling, incident response.  
    When used: Attached to MSA/SOW or DPA.
12. **Incident Response & Breach Notification Addendum**  
    Purpose: Defines escalation timelines, triage responsibilities, communication channels, and forensic cooperation.  
    When used: Enterprise procurement, regulated clients, or carrier/TPA workflows.
13. **Record Retention & Legal Hold Policy**  
    Purpose: Standardizes retention, deletion, hold events, and immutable audit record handling.  
    When used: Internal governance and contractual audit obligations.

### D. AI Governance + Responsible Use
14. **AI Use Disclosure + Human Oversight Addendum (MODULE AI-1)**  
    Purpose: Discloses AI usage, role boundaries, and mandatory human review checkpoints.  
    When used: All AI-enabled services.
15. **AI Output Reliability & Non-Reliance Addendum (MODULE AI-2)**  
    Purpose: Clarifies AI outputs are assistive, may contain errors, and require independent professional review.  
    When used: Any generated report, narrative, recommendation, or score.
16. **AI Training Data/IP & Non-Infringement Posture Statement (MODULE AI-3)**  
    Purpose: Documents data provenance controls and restrictions on unlawful datasets.  
    When used: Procurement security/legal diligence and customer contracting.
17. **Algorithmic Fairness & Monitoring Standard (MODULE AI-4)**  
    Purpose: Defines discrimination-risk checks, drift/bias monitoring, and escalation procedures.  
    When used: High-risk or consequential-use cases.
18. **AI Incident Reporting & Model Failure Escalation SOP (MODULE AI-5)**  
    Purpose: Sets triggers and response workflow for model failure or harmful output events.  
    When used: Internal operations + enterprise audit requests.
19. **AI Logging, Explainability, and Audit Access Addendum (MODULE AI-6)**  
    Purpose: Establishes traceability and controlled customer/regulator audit access.  
    When used: Enterprise and regulated environments.
20. **AI-Generated Content Labeling & Provenance Policy (MODULE AI-7)**  
    Purpose: Labels synthetic text/image/video and preserves generation metadata/provenance.  
    When used: Any customer-facing AI-generated content.
21. **AI Vendor/Subprocessor Management Standard (MODULE AI-8)**  
    Purpose: Requires due diligence, contractual flow-down controls, and periodic reassessment of AI vendors.  
    When used: Third-party LLM/model/service dependencies.
22. **Customer AI Responsibilities & Prompt Safety Terms (MODULE AI-9)**  
    Purpose: Sets prohibited uses, prompt hygiene, user accountability, and safe-operation obligations.  
    When used: AI feature enablement in customer contracts.

### E. Field Operations + Claims Workflows
23. **Contractor / Inspection Services Agreement**  
    Purpose: Governs field capture, photo/drone evidence integrity, metadata requirements, and chain-of-custody.  
    When used: Third-party inspectors or field ops support.
24. **Insurance Cooperation & Documentation Agreement**  
    Purpose: Permits documentation support and cooperation while avoiding unauthorized "representation" or public adjusting.  
    When used: Customer workflows involving insurer communications/doc packages.
25. **Evidence Integrity & Chain-of-Custody Protocol**  
    Purpose: Controls capture standards, timestamping, tamper-evidence, and transfer logs.  
    When used: Claims disputes, litigation hold, carrier challenges.
26. **Call Recording & Communication Consent Notice**  
    Purpose: Captures consent and state-law handling requirements for recorded calls/interactions.  
    When used: Any recorded support or claims-related calls.

### F. Consumer + Marketing Compliance
27. **Marketing Claims Substantiation Policy (AI/ROI/Accuracy)**  
    Purpose: Requires evidence substantiation and approval workflows for performance claims.  
    When used: Sales scripts, website copy, demos, proposals.
28. **Anti-Rebating / Inducement Compliance Addendum**  
    Purpose: Limits incentive structures and messaging that could trigger insurance inducement concerns.  
    When used: Promotions, referral campaigns, contractor-customer offers.
29. **Door-to-Door / Home Solicitation Compliance Notice Pack**  
    Purpose: Covers cancellation/rescission requirements and mandatory notices by jurisdiction.  
    When used: In-home or door-to-door sales channels.
30. **E-Sign and Electronic Records Consent**  
    Purpose: Validates electronic signature and records consent for contract and workflow artifacts.  
    When used: Digital onboarding and transaction flow.

### G. Corporate Governance + Operational Hygiene
31. **Subprocessor List & Change Notification Policy**  
    Purpose: Maintains transparent subprocessor disclosures and notice mechanics.  
    When used: DPA compliance.
32. **Compliance Training & Attestation Policy**  
    Purpose: Requires recurring training, certification, and acknowledgment for employees/contractors.  
    When used: Internal compliance program.
33. **Regulatory Inquiry & Audit Response Procedure**  
    Purpose: Assigns ownership, deadlines, and response QA for regulator, insurer, or litigation requests.  
    When used: External inquiries and audits.

## 4) Generation Order + Dependencies
1. **MSA** (master risk and commercial framework).  
2. **DPA + Security Exhibit + Subprocessor Policy** (data governance baseline).  
3. **Terms of Use + Privacy Policy + AUP** (public surface alignment).  
4. **SLA + API Terms** (operational and technical commitments).  
5. **AI Addenda (AI-1…AI-9)** (AI-specific control layer).  
6. **Field/Claims docs** (inspection, cooperation, evidence chain) after core contract language is stable.  
7. **Marketing/consumer compliance docs** (substantiation, anti-rebating, solicitation notices) once GTM channels are confirmed.

Dependency highlights:
- DPA references Security Exhibit and Subprocessor Policy.  
- AI Reliability clauses depend on base warranty/disclaimer structure in MSA/Terms.  
- Insurance Cooperation language should inherit jurisdiction-specific forks from state notice packs.

## Attorney Review Notes (State/Region Fork Selection)
- **[STATE-SPECIFIC OPTION — AOB/cancellation/notice forks]:** Activate when customer operations include assignment-of-benefits, emergency services contracting, home solicitation sales, or rescission-triggering consumer contracts in the target state(s).  
- **[STATE-SPECIFIC OPTION — recording consent forks]:** Use one-party or all-party call recording language based on state law for support and claims calls.  
- **[STATE-SPECIFIC OPTION — insurance cooperation language]:** Keep "cooperation/documentation support" language by default; use any representational wording only where licensure and jurisdictional rules explicitly permit.  
- **[EU OPTION — AI transparency labeling]:** Include Article 50 style transparency clauses when users/data/customers are in EU scope or when AI-generated content is provided to EU users.
